# Lead Generation Campaign — PixelForge Web Studio

**Client (fictional):** PixelForge — a small web design studio
**Objective:** Generate qualified leads for website-design projects via a landing page + paid/organic social.
**Prepared by:** Ajvi Dylgjeri — Sqrock IT Digital Marketing Internship, Phase 1 · Task 2

---

## 1. Goal
Capture **50 qualified leads in 30 days** at a target cost-per-lead (CPL) under €4, via a dedicated landing page and a lead magnet.

## 2. Funnel
**Awareness** (social ads/posts) → **Interest** (landing page) → **Lead** (free audit form) → **Nurture** (email) → **Client**

## 3. Lead magnet (the offer)
> **"Free 15-minute website audit + a 1-page action plan."**

Low-friction, high-value, and naturally qualifies prospects (only people who want a better website opt in).

## 4. Landing page structure
See `landing-page/index.html` for the built page. Sections, in order:
1. **Hero** — clear headline, subhead, single CTA ("Get my free audit")
2. **Social proof** — logos / stats / rating strip
3. **Problem → solution** — pains they feel, how PixelForge fixes them
4. **What you get** — 3–4 benefit cards
5. **How it works** — 3 steps
6. **Testimonials** — short quotes
7. **Lead form** — name, email, website URL, biggest challenge (minimal fields)
8. **FAQ** — handles objections
9. **Final CTA** — repeat the offer

**Conversion principles applied:** one primary CTA repeated, minimal form fields, benefit-led copy, friction removed, trust signals above and below the fold.

## 5. 5 ad / post ideas (to drive traffic)
1. **Problem hook (Reel/ad):** "Your website has 5 seconds to make an impression. Here's what's costing you customers 👇" → CTA to free audit.
2. **Before/after carousel:** redesign transformation → "Want results like this? Free audit in bio."
3. **Tip post:** "3 quick fixes that make any site load faster" (value first) → soft CTA.
4. **Testimonial ad:** client quote + result ("+30% enquiries") → "Book your free audit."
5. **Urgency/limited slots:** "We take 5 audit slots a week — 2 left. Claim yours." → CTA.

**Targeting (paid):** local SMBs, 25–55, interests: small business, entrepreneurship, Shopify/WordPress; 10–20km radius. Start €5/day/ad, kill losers after 3 days, scale winners.

## 6. Nurture (email, 3 touches)
1. Deliver the audit + action plan.
2. Case study + "common website mistakes."
3. Soft offer: "Ready to fix it? Here's how we'd work together" + booking link.

## 7. KPIs
Impressions, CTR, landing-page conversion rate (target ≥ 10%), leads, CPL, audit-booked rate, lead→client rate. Optimise the weakest funnel stage first.
