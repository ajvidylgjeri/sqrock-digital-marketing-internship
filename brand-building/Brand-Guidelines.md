# Brand Building Project — Verdé

**Brand (fictional):** Verdé — an indoor plant & lifestyle shop
**Objective:** Create a cohesive brand identity and a social-launch concept for a new local brand.
**Prepared by:** Ajvi Dylgjeri — Sqrock IT Digital Marketing Internship, Phase 1 · Task 3

---

## 1. Brand strategy

**Mission:** Bring a little more green — and calm — into everyday city living.

**Vision:** To be the friendliest first step into plant parenthood for people in Tirana.

**Brand personality:** Calm · friendly · knowledgeable · optimistic. (If Verdé were a person: the patient friend who always keeps their plants alive and happily tells you how.)

**Positioning statement:**
> For city dwellers who want greenery without the guesswork, Verdé is the plant shop that pairs easy-care plants with friendly, no-jargon guidance — so anyone can keep something alive and thriving.

**Tagline:** *Grow easy.*

## 2. Target audience
- Urban 22–40, apartments, first-time plant owners
- Gift buyers (plants as gifts)
- Home/office stylists wanting low-maintenance greenery

## 3. Logo
See `assets/logo.svg` (and `assets/logo-mark.svg` for the icon-only mark).
- **Wordmark:** "Verdé" in a soft rounded serif, with a leaf replacing the accent on the "é".
- **Mark:** a simple leaf/sprout in a circle — works as an app icon and profile picture.
- **Clear space:** keep at least the height of the leaf around the logo.
- **Don'ts:** don't stretch, don't recolour outside the palette, don't add shadows.

## 4. Colour palette
| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|------|------|-----|-----|
| Primary | Fern | `#3f7d4e` | Logo, headings, buttons |
| Deep | Forest | `#24402b` | Text on light, dark sections |
| Accent | Terracotta | `#c8724a` | Highlights, CTAs |
| Light | Cream | `#f3efe6` | Backgrounds |
| Soft | Sage | `#cdd9c3` | Cards, dividers |

## 5. Typography
- **Headings:** a rounded serif (e.g. *Fraunces*) — warm, organic.
- **Body:** a clean sans (e.g. *Inter*) — legible, modern.
- Generous line height and spacing for an airy, calm feel.

## 6. Voice & tone
- Warm and encouraging, never preachy. "You've got this."
- Plain language — no plant Latin unless we explain it.
- Short, helpful, a little playful. Emoji in moderation (🌱🪴).

**Examples**
- ✅ "New to plants? Start here — three that are almost impossible to kill."
- ❌ "Acquire horticultural specimens of superior resilience."

## 7. Social launch concept (first 2 weeks)
| Day | Post | Goal |
|-----|------|------|
| 1 | Brand reveal — logo + "Grow easy." | Announce |
| 2 | "Meet Verdé" — our story Reel | Connect |
| 3 | "3 plants you can't kill" carousel | Educate/save |
| 4 | Poll: "What kills your plants?" | Engage |
| 5 | Product spotlight — the Pothos | Promote |
| 6 | Care tip Reel — watering 101 | Educate |
| 7 | UGC/giveaway launch | Grow |

See `social-mockups.html` for post designs and `landing-concept.html` for the homepage concept.

## 8. Success metrics
Follower growth, saves/shares (education content), giveaway entries, website clicks, launch-week sales.
