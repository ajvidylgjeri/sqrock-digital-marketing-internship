# Local Business Growth Challenge — Bloom Café

**Client (fictional):** Bloom Café — a cosy specialty coffee shop in Tirana
**Objective:** Grow local awareness and foot traffic, and build an engaged Instagram/Facebook community over 30 days.
**Prepared by:** Ajvi Dylgjeri — Sqrock IT Digital Marketing Internship, Phase 1 · Task 1

---

## 1. Goals (SMART)
- **Awareness:** +40% Instagram reach in 30 days.
- **Community:** +500 local followers (Tirana, 18–35).
- **Foot traffic:** 150 redemptions of a "mention this post" offer.
- **Engagement:** average engagement rate ≥ 6% on posts.

## 2. Target audience
| Segment | Who | Hook |
|---------|-----|------|
| Students | UNYT / nearby universities | Study-friendly space, Wi-Fi, student discount |
| Young professionals | 25–35, work nearby | Quick quality coffee, loyalty card |
| Weekend crowd | Couples, friends | Brunch, aesthetic space, photo spots |

## 3. Positioning & tone
Warm, friendly, a little playful. "Your neighbourhood coffee corner." Visual style: natural light, earthy tones, latte art, greenery.

## 4. Channels
- **Instagram** (primary) — feed + Reels + Stories
- **Facebook** (secondary) — events, reviews, local reach
- **Google Business Profile** — reviews & local search

## 5. Content pillars (the "3 E's + 1")
1. **Educate** — coffee tips, "how we brew" (20%)
2. **Entertain** — Reels, behind-the-scenes, memes (30%)
3. **Engage** — polls, questions, user photos (25%)
4. **Promote** — offers, new menu items, loyalty (25%)

## 6. 10 post ideas (with caption copy)

1. **Reel — "A latte in 15 seconds"** (Educate)
   *Caption:* "From bean to bloom ☕ Watch your morning come together. Which one's your go-to? #BloomCafe #TiranaCoffee"

2. **Carousel — "5 ways to order your coffee"** (Educate)
   *Caption:* "Flat white or cortado? Save this for your next visit 👀 Tag the friend who always steals your drink."

3. **Photo — Latte art close-up** (Entertain)
   *Caption:* "We put a little love in every cup 💛 Come find your favourite corner."

4. **Story poll — "Oat milk vs regular?"** (Engage)
   *Caption sticker:* Quick poll + "Winner goes on our specials board!"

5. **Reel — Behind the counter morning rush** (Entertain)
   *Caption:* "5 minutes before we open ⏰ The calm before the espresso storm."

6. **UGC repost — customer's photo** (Engage)
   *Caption:* "Spotted at Bloom 📸 Tag us in your shots for a chance to be featured!"

7. **Offer post — "Student Tuesdays"** (Promote)
   *Caption:* "Show your student ID on Tuesdays = 20% off ☕📚 Study fuel, sorted. #UNYT"

8. **Carousel — New seasonal menu** (Promote)
   *Caption:* "Autumn is here and so is our Maple Oat Latte 🍁 Swipe to meet the new menu."

9. **Photo — The space / reading nook** (Entertain)
   *Caption:* "Good coffee, better corners. Where do you sit? 🪴"

10. **Engagement post — "This or that"** (Engage)
    *Caption:* "Sweet or strong? Comment your pick 👇 We'll feature the top answer tomorrow."

*(Bonus 11–12: a loyalty-card announcement and a Google-review request Story.)*

## 7. Hashtag strategy
Mix of reach sizes (don't repeat the same block every time):
- **Local:** #TiranaCoffee #Tirana #KafeTirana #VisitTirana
- **Niche:** #SpecialtyCoffee #LatteArt #CoffeeLovers #ThirdWaveCoffee
- **Branded:** #BloomCafe #BloomMoments
- **Community:** #CoffeeShopVibes #StudyCafe #CoffeeTime

Use 8–12 per post, rotate sets.

## 8. 30-day posting schedule (rhythm)
| Day type | Instagram | Stories |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| Mon | Promote (offer) | 2–3 |
| Tue | Educate (Reel) | 2–3 (student Tue) |
| Wed | Engage (poll/this-or-that) | 2–3 |
| Thu | Entertain (BTS Reel) | 2–3 |
| Fri | Promote (weekend teaser) | 3–4 |
| Sat | Entertain (space/UGC) | 3–4 |
| Sun | Engage (question) | 2–3 |

**Best times (local):** 8–9am, 1–2pm, 7–9pm. ~4–5 feed posts/week + daily Stories + 2–3 Reels/week.

## 9. Offer mechanic (foot-traffic driver)
"**Mention this post** for a free upgrade to any size this week." Trackable at the till (tally sheet) → measures social → foot-traffic conversion.

## 10. KPIs & tracking
Reach, follower growth, engagement rate, Reel plays, saves/shares, offer redemptions, Google reviews. Review weekly; double down on the best-performing pillar.
